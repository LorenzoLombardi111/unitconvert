How to Convert Pounds to Kilograms

The formula to convert pounds to kilograms is straightforward: multiply the number of pounds by 0.453592. The exact conversion factor, established by international agreement in 1959, is 0.45359237. For most practical purposes, 0.453592 provides more than enough precision.

The formula is:

kilograms = pounds × 0.453592

For a worked example, let us convert 150 pounds to kilograms. Multiply 150 by 0.453592 to get 68.0389 kg. This means a person who weighs 150 lbs weighs approximately 68.04 kilograms. Note that 1 pound equals exactly 0.45359237 kg by international agreement, so there is no ambiguity in this conversion.

If you need to go the other direction, divide kilograms by 0.453592, or equivalently multiply by 2.20462. Our calculator handles both directions so you never have to remember which way the math goes.

When Do You Need to Convert lbs to kg?

Pounds-to-kilograms conversion comes up more often than you might expect. Here are the most common situations where you will need it:

  • Traveling internationally — Airlines outside the United States display luggage allowances in kilograms. Knowing that your 50-pound suitcase weighs 22.7 kg can save you from surprise overweight fees at check-in.
  • Medical records — Most countries record patient weight in kilograms. If you are visiting a doctor abroad or reading medical literature, you will need to convert your weight from pounds to kilograms for accurate dosing and record-keeping.
  • Fitness and weightlifting — Olympic barbells and competition weights are measured in kilograms. A standard Olympic plate weighs 20 kg (about 44.1 lbs), and understanding the conversion helps you track progress across different gym equipment.
  • Shipping internationally — Freight carriers and postal services worldwide use kilograms for package weight. Accurate conversion ensures you calculate shipping costs correctly and fill out customs forms properly.
  • Scientific calculations — The International System of Units (SI) uses the kilogram as the base unit of mass. Any scientific work, from chemistry to physics to engineering, requires weight in kilograms for formulas and calculations to work correctly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many kilograms are in a pound?

1 lb = 0.453592 kg (exactly 0.45359237 kg). This conversion factor was established by international agreement in 1959 and is used worldwide.

What is the formula for lbs to kg?

Multiply pounds by 0.453592. For example, 10 lbs × 0.453592 = 4.53592 kg. This gives you an accurate result for virtually any practical application.

How do I convert kg back to lbs?

Multiply kilograms by 2.20462, or use our kg to lbs converter. For example, 70 kg × 2.20462 = 154.324 lbs.

Is 1 kg exactly 2.2 pounds?

Not exactly. 1 kg = 2.20462262185 lbs. Using 2.2 introduces a 0.2% error. For rough estimates this is fine, but for anything requiring precision you should use the full conversion factor.

Why do some countries use pounds and others kilograms?

Only the US, Liberia, and Myanmar officially use pounds. The rest of the world uses kilograms as part of the metric system (SI). The metric system was adopted internationally for its simplicity and consistency in scientific and commercial applications.
